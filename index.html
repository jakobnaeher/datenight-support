Frequently Asked Questions

How does Date Night work?

Date Night is an event-based social app where people host date ideas (a venue, time, and activity) and others can request to join. Every request starts with a real plan, not just a profile.

How do I host a date?

Tap the "Host" tab, choose a category (dinner, coffee, adventure, etc.), pick a venue, set a date and time, and add a description. Your date event will appear in the Explore feed for others to discover.

How do I join someone's date?

Browse the Explore feed, find a date event you like, and tap "Join This Date." You can add a personal note to your request. The host will review your request and decide whether to accept.

How do I block or report someone?

You can report or block a user from profiles, date posts, map/list venue views, conversations, and message menus. Blocking hides that user's profile, events, and conversations from your Date Night experience.

Is my location shared?

No. Your location is not requested, displayed, or shared with other users. Every in-app venue map requires a manual tap to reveal venue pins, and date details may show the selected venue name for the plan.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Me > Settings > scroll to Account Management and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your attendee details, posts, confirmed date chats, and messages, subject to limited legal and safety retention exceptions in our Privacy Policy.

How do subscriptions work?

Date Night offers optional Premium subscriptions for unlimited date posts through the App Store. All subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. You can cancel anytime in your device Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

How is my data protected?

We take your privacy seriously. We do not sell personal information, and your location is never shown to other users. See our Privacy Policy in the app for full details.

Safety

Your Safety Matters

Always meet in public places. Tell a friend where you're going. Use the in-app safety checklist before every date. If you feel unsafe, contact someone you trust or local emergency services directly. Report suspicious behavior from the profile or chat menu.
