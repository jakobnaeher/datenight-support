Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Date Night, Inc. operates the Date Night mobile application. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including name, email address, phone number, age, gender, preferences, photos, profile details, prompts, hobbies, neighborhood, date posts, join requests, messages, optional voice messages if enabled, reports, blocks, account deletion requests, and support requests.

If you use optional identity verification, Persona may process government ID information, a selfie, and verification results as our service provider. Date Night stores verification status, not your government ID images.

We collect subscription status and purchase confirmation data from Apple. Apple processes payment details; Date Night does not collect or store payment card numbers or bank account information.

Optional safety contact information is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to Date Night.

Location Data

You may provide a neighborhood or selected city. Date Night does not request device location, automatically check you in, continuously track your GPS location, display your live location, or share your location with other users.

The Explore map displays venue locations for date posts, not user location pins, and requires a fresh manual tap before venue pins appear. Date detail screens may display the selected date venue so users can understand the plan. Venue searches may use Google Places, and directions may open Apple Maps.

How We Use Information

  • Operate, secure, and improve Date Night.
  • Create and manage accounts, profiles, date posts, join requests, confirmations, conversations, reports, blocks, and account deletion.
  • Show relevant date events and venue information.
  • Send service notifications such as requests, confirmations, messages, reminders, and safety notices.
  • Process subscriptions through Apple's in-app purchase system.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our terms.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to support or privacy requests.

Profile Visibility and Photos

Your public profile information, date posts, selected photos, neighborhood, and verification status may be visible to eligible users inside the app. Messages are visible only to conversation participants. Reports are reviewed for safety and moderation.

Profile photos may be stored using cloud storage and delivered through public media URLs so they can load reliably in the app. Do not upload photos or profile details you do not want other eligible users to see.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use third-party advertising identifiers.

We share information with service providers needed to operate the app, including Supabase for hosting, authentication, database, and storage; Persona for optional identity verification; Google Places for venue search; and Apple for App Store distribution, subscriptions, Sign in with Apple, and notifications.

We may disclose information when required by law, to enforce our terms, to investigate safety or security issues, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

Retention

We retain account data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize personal information within 30 days, except where limited retention is necessary for safety, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or enforcement. Safety reports and moderation records may be retained for up to three years.

Your Choices and Rights

You can update profile information in the app, manage notification permissions in device settings and in-app settings, cancel subscriptions through Apple, block or report users, and delete your account from Settings. You may also request access, correction, deletion, portability, or other applicable privacy rights by emailing us.

California and other U.S. state privacy laws may provide additional rights, including the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing. Date Night does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards such as TLS, authenticated access controls, row-level database security, and limited internal access. No system is completely secure, so you should protect your account credentials and contact us if you believe your account has been compromised.

Children

Date Night is only for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that a minor has created an account, we will delete the account.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the app or on this page with an updated date.
