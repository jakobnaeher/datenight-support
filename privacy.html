Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly: name, email, phone number, photos, profile details, preferences, date posts, join requests, messages, reports, blocks, and support requests. Optional emergency contact details are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to Date Night. We do not continuously track your precise GPS location.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to operate the app, show relevant date events, facilitate hosting and join requests, provide safety features, process reports, send notifications, manage subscriptions, and improve app reliability.

Location Data

We use your neighborhood selection and, if you grant permission, device location to show nearby events and map results. Only venue locations are displayed on the map. Your exact location is never shared with other users.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the app, including hosting, authentication, storage, maps and venue search, identity verification, phone verification, payments through Apple, support, safety, and analytics.

Your Rights

You can request access to your data or delete your account from Settings within the app. Account deletion removes your profile, posts, connections, and messages, subject to limited legal, security, and safety retention exceptions.
