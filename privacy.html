We collect information you provide directly: name, photos, date preferences, and profile details. We do not continuously track your precise GPS location.

Your information is used to show you relevant date events near you, facilitate connections between users, and improve the app experience.

We use your neighborhood selection to show nearby events. Only venue locations are displayed on the map. Your exact location is never shared with other users.

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the app (authentication, hosting).
